# Fresh Frame privacy policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Fresh Frame is a video review app published by Steve Long. It was previously named BJJ Telestrator.

## Your videos and reviews

Videos you select, drawings, review notes, and microphone recordings are processed and stored on your device. Fresh Frame does not upload this content to a developer-operated server. No account is required. The app does not contain advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics services.

Microphone access is used only when you choose to record commentary. The system Photos picker lets you choose videos; permission to add to Photos is requested when you choose to save an exported video. You can manage permissions in iPhone Settings.

## Sharing and backups

You choose whether to export an MP4, share a file, or save an editable project backup. Files shared with another app or saved to a cloud provider are handled under that provider's policies. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Reviews remain on your device until you remove them. Reviews moved to Recently Deleted can be restored. Deleting the app can delete its local files, so export project backups first if you want to keep editable reviews.

## Support and diagnostics

If you contact us or voluntarily send a diagnostic report, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to investigate your request. Do not include private footage or personal information in public GitHub issues. Apple may separately process purchase, download, and diagnostic information under its own privacy settings and policies.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the [project support page](https://github.com/BakedChicken77/bjj-telestrator/blob/main/docs/SUPPORT.md). Policy changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
